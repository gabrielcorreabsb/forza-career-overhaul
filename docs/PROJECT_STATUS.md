# Project status — where the career overhaul is and where it goes

## 1. Goal

Bring the career of Forza Motorsport (2023) closer to the career of Forza
Motorsport 4: a **permanent event list** where nothing expires and no car is
missed for being late, the **right car for each event** instead of a handful of
open-class catch-alls, and a **denser entry path** at classes E and D. On top of
that: an AI field that races honestly (no rubber band, a real multi-class grid)
and **one-make series** instead of a manufacturer-affinity system the game keeps
on its server.

Forza Motorsport 4 is a design reference only; nothing from it is distributed
here (see [THIRD_PARTY](THIRD_PARTY.md) and `NOTICE.md`).

## 3. What we learned

1. **The cache is the truth, and it can be edited in place.** Keep the blob name,
   update `Size`/`Checksum`, re-encrypt with the current payload as template; the
   game accepts it and untouched entries stay byte-identical.
2. **The AI grid is not in the series record.** An `AIField` XML pins the field,
   and that file lives only in the install archive — a different tool and a
   different set of files.
3. **Vanilla grids are one recipe, and it is narrow.** 522 events draw from a
   pool with the AI 10–50 PI below the player; only 85 events (23 series, mostly
   endurance) use a fixed list, which is what makes a real multi-class grid
   possible.
4. **The career is top-heavy.** 209 of 711 cars are in no acceptlist, E (31/50)
   and D (50/106) are the worst served, while 245 of 627 events sit at
   `TargetPI` 900 or 800.
5. **Hidden caps live on the event, not on the series.** One inherited
   `TargetPI = 700` held a whole series at A700 while the override said 999.

Full log, with the evidence and a status for each finding:
[DISCOVERIES](guides/DISCOVERIES.md).

## 4. Where we are going

State labels: **viable** (the mechanism is known and the data supports it),
**to test** (plausible, needs a controlled run in game), **not possible** (the
value is decided by the game or its server, not by the career data).

### Now

| item | why | state | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Permanent event list / series archive** | The career has no gates and no expiry left, except one old ladder that is a copy of a live one; the timed content is on the Featured side (62 series, none carrying complete car, AI and event lists). An archive cup whose series are re-parented with fresh ids and synthesized lists gives the whole set a permanent home. | viable (re-parenting is proven; synthesizing lists is the open part) | [CAREER_MODEL](CAREER_MODEL.md), [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) 14 |
| **Car × event matrix** | 209 cars are guaranteed no slot. A matrix that lists every car in at least one event turns the catalogue into the career: filter by make, year and class to generate acceptlists, then one series per group. | viable (generator takes a filter; drivetrain, aspiration and country are not in the data) | [CAREER_AUDIT](CAREER_AUDIT.md) §6.2, [CARS_AND_PI](CARS_AND_PI.md) |
| **Entry path at E and D** | The entry classes are the ones a new profile owns and the ones the career serves worst. Four to six short series that only accept E/D cars, with a band that keeps the AI in the same class, closes the gap. | viable | [CAREER_AUDIT](CAREER_AUDIT.md) §6.2–6.3, [CUSTOM_RACE](guides/CUSTOM_RACE.md) §5 |

### Next — to test

| item | why | state | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Real qualifying** | The event schema carries `QualificationOptions` (limit type, number of laps, one-shot) and a skip-lap switch, all at their defaults. If a session can be made to run and set the grid, a race weekend becomes possible. | to test | [RACE_SYSTEM](RACE_SYSTEM.md), [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) 1 |
| **AI without the rubber band** | `EnableDifficultyCatchUp/SlowDown` is `true` on all 178 series; play so far suggests the pace is *not* flattened, but there is no lap table to prove either way. | to test | [DISCOVERIES](guides/DISCOVERIES.md) D26, [AI_AND_GRIDS](AI_AND_GRIDS.md) |
| **Pit stops, tyre wear and long races** | Fuel use and tyre wear already exist per series (40 series run `FuelTire` damage, wear scalar 1–6); what is missing is race length, because laps come from a field that disagrees with the override on 600 of 627 events. | to test (wear: viable; long races depend on the lap-count question) | [RACE_SYSTEM](RACE_SYSTEM.md) §2.3, [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) 2 |
| **Championship scoring** | `PointSpread` is one constant 24-value table on every event, so no series has its own points shape. Unknown whether a different table survives into a championship. | to test | [RACE_SYSTEM](RACE_SYSTEM.md), [CAREER_AUDIT](CAREER_AUDIT.md) §6.6 |

### Later

- **Grids above 24 cars** — every vanilla document asks for 23 AI + the player;
  a bigger field needs an AI field document and has no precedent. State: to test
  ([OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) 8).
- **Own art per series** — posters, banners and intro videos are inherited from
  the donor series today; replacing them means repacking art assets.
- **The grid recipe on ordinary series** — the pool/band work is proven on one
  custom series; applying it to a handful of vanilla series is the next step to
  make it a career-wide rule.
- **Other languages** — new strings go into the pt-BR table only; the other 19
  locales have no keys yet.

### Not planned

| item | why not |
|---|---|
| **Manufacturer affinity** | The progression value lives in the game and its server; the career data cannot grant or store it. The substitute is the **one-make series**: same make, different models and eras, which the generator already supports. |
| **Tyre compounds** | The data exposes wear and fuel, not a compound choice. |
| **Ghost braking / driving aids of the AI** | These follow the engine, not the career content. |

## 5. How to help

- **Run the pipeline on a copy** and report what breaks:
  [GETTING_STARTED](en/GETTING_STARTED.md) takes about 15 minutes and never
  touches the game.
- **Build a series** from a spec and open an issue with the spec: the interesting
  cases are the ones this repository has not tried (a decade, a country, a
  one-make grid, an entry-class ladder).
- **Settle an open question**: each item in [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)
  names the experiment that decides it. A result with the run recorded is worth
  more than a guess.
- **Improve a guide** with a symptom you hit and its cause:
  [TROUBLESHOOTING](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) is the collected version.
