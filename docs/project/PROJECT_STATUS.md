# Project status — where the career overhaul is and where it goes

## 1. Goal

The career we are assembling, stage by stage, is described in
[CAREER_MOD](../guides/CAREER_MOD.md); this page tracks the state and the plan.

Bring the career of Forza Motorsport (2023) closer to the career of Forza
Motorsport 4: a **permanent event list** where nothing expires and no car is
missed for being late, the **right car for each event** instead of a handful of
open-class catch-alls, and a **denser entry path** at classes E and D. On top of
that: a **race weekend** (practice that sets the **AI's** grid, longer races with wear
and fuel), an AI field that races honestly (no rubber band, a real multi-class
grid) and **one-make series** instead of a manufacturer-affinity system the game
keeps on its server.

Forza Motorsport 4 is a design reference only; nothing from it is distributed
here (see [THIRD_PARTY](THIRD_PARTY.md) and `NOTICE.md`).

## 3. What we learned

1. **The cache is the truth, and it can be edited in place.** Keep the blob name,
   update `Size`/`Checksum`, re-encrypt with the current payload as template; the
   game accepts it and untouched entries stay byte-identical.
2. **The AI grid is not in the series record.** An `AIField` XML pins the field,
   and that file lives only in the install archive — a different tool and a
   different set of files. No other mode uses the career's grids.
3. **The override decides the race, the event decides the cap.** Race length
   comes from the event override; the class/PI cap a player sees comes from the
   event's `TargetPI`.
4. **The client reads its feature flags from the cache.** One value switched off
   a whole screen that the career data could not remove; per-mode lists in the
   same file can target only the career.
5. **The career is top-heavy.** 209 of 711 cars are in no acceptlist, E (31/50)
   and D (50/106) are the worst served, while 245 of 627 events sit at
   `TargetPI` 900 or 800.

6. **A session generator decides the human's start slot.** With rank order,
   a start index acts as a cut: below it the player starts first, above it
   last, whatever his lap; with qualifying order the slot is a fixed 7th. The
   wager screen's pick is overridden (observed, one run per setting).
7. **The generator's car restriction is enforced.** A Racers Cup generator with
   a strict class-R bucket refused a class C car the series allowed and
   accepted a class R one (observed).
8. **A mandatory pit stop is enforced on the AI too.** On a 2-lap race many AI
   cars were disqualified for not stopping; on 4 laps all of them stopped
   (observed).
9. **A race needs AI.** An empty AI document ran the practice but crashed the
   race (observed, one run).

Full log, with the evidence and a status for each finding:
[DISCOVERIES](DISCOVERIES.md).

## 4. Where we are going

State labels: **viable** (the mechanism is known and the data supports it),
**to test** (plausible, needs a controlled run in game), **not possible** (the
value is decided by the game or its server, not by the content data).

### Now — in-game verification, starting with qualifying

| item | why | state | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Race weekend** | Both halves of the split grid are now known: the practice classification orders the **AI**, and the start-position wager places the **player** — with the screen on it suggests a slot and honours the picked one, so the slot is a lever rather than a constant. The wager screen and the AI order were checked together on the bench series, so the next step is a **real qualifying session**, with the one-shot lap variant, the AI behaviour during practice and longer races with wear and fuel behind it. | to test (AI order and the wager as the player's slot: verified, one run each; the rest: to test) | [RACE_SYSTEM](../reference/RACE_SYSTEM.md), [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) 16 |
| **AI without the rubber band** | Catch-up/slow-down are on in every vanilla series; the session generators already have rubber banding off, so the per-series behaviour flags are the lever left to test. | to test | [AI_GRIDS](../guides/AI_GRIDS.md), [DISCOVERIES](DISCOVERIES.md) D26 |
| **Career tyre compound** | A per-mode list in the client flags sets the default tyre for three online modes but not for the career; adding a career item is one entry. | to test | [MODDING_SURFACE](../reference/MODDING_SURFACE.md) |

### Next — viable

| item | why | state | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Car × event matrix** | 209 cars are guaranteed no slot. A matrix that lists every car in at least one event turns the catalogue into the career: filter by make, year and class, then one series per group. If a series' numeric limits (PI, year, power) filter on their own, no long acceptlists are needed. | viable (numeric limits: to test) | [CAREER_AUDIT](CAREER_AUDIT.md) §6.2, [CARS_AND_PI](../reference/CARS_AND_PI.md) |
| **Entry path at E and D** | The entry classes are the ones a new profile owns and the ones the career serves worst. Four to six short series that only accept E/D cars, with a band that keeps the AI in the same class. | viable | [CAREER_AUDIT](CAREER_AUDIT.md) §6.2–6.3, [CUSTOM_RACE](../guides/CUSTOM_RACE.md) |
| **Permanent event list / series archive** | The timed content is on the Featured side (62 series). An archive cup whose series are re-parented with fresh ids and synthesized lists gives it a permanent home. | viable (re-parenting is proven; synthesizing lists is the open part) | [CAREER_MODEL](../reference/CAREER_MODEL.md), [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) 14 |
| **Weather, time of day and race rules per event** | The career's session generators are used by the career only, so their weather, time-of-day and regulation fields are one value per event. | viable | [MODDING_SURFACE](../reference/MODDING_SURFACE.md) |

### Later

- **Championship objectives** for career series — the documents exist only for
  Featured series, and adding one needs a tool that creates a new cache entry.
- **Grids above 24 cars** — every vanilla document asks for 23 AI + the player;
  no precedent ([OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) 8).
- **Visual settings per track and render presets** — readable files in the
  install; they need an install file-swap tool with backup and restore.
- **Own art per series** — posters, banners and intro videos are inherited from
  the donor series today.
- **Other languages** — new strings go into the pt-BR table only; the other 19
  locales have no keys yet.

### Not planned

| item | why not |
|---|---|
| **Manufacturer affinity** | The progression value lives in the game and its server; the career data cannot grant or store it. The substitute is the **one-make series**. |
| **Physics, prizes, unlocks, account economy** | Global (they change online play or the account), and the files are encrypted containers the project does not write. |
| **Global AI or difficulty switches** | They change every mode, multiplayer included. |
| **Ghost braking / driving aids of the AI** | These follow the engine, not the content. |

## 5. How to help

- **Run the pipeline on a copy** and report what breaks:
  [GETTING_STARTED](../en/GETTING_STARTED.md) takes about 15 minutes and never
  touches the game.
- **Build a series** from a spec and open an issue with the spec: the interesting
  cases are the ones this repository has not tried (a decade, a country, a
  one-make grid, an entry-class ladder).
- **Settle an open question**: each item in [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)
  names the experiment that decides it. A result with the run recorded is worth
  more than a guess.
- **Improve a guide** with a symptom you hit and its cause:
  [TROUBLESHOOTING](../guides/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) is the collected version.
