# Code of conduct

Be useful and be kind. This is a small fan project; the goal is a good Forza
Motorsport career for everyone who wants one.

- Assume good faith, argue about facts and evidence, not about people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Respect other people's time: one idea per issue or PR, exact commands and
  outputs, no demands for support or deadlines.
- Respect the rights holders: no requests for, or sharing of, game files, keys or
  third-party tools ([SECURITY](SECURITY.md)); no help circumventing online
  protections.

Maintainers may edit, hide or close contributions that break these rules and may
block repeat offenders. To report a conduct problem privately, use **Security → Report a vulnerability**
on the repository page and say it is a conduct report.
